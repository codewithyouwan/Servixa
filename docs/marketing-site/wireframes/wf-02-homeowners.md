## Hierarchy & conversion notes

- Only one primary CTA appears in the hero (no "I'm a Contractor" button competing here) — a homeowner-audience page shouldn't split attention with a CTA meant for a different visitor.
- The "What You Can Build/Fix" category grid does double duty as UI and as an internal SEO link hub into the cost-guide pages — placed mid-page, after trust/value is established but before the visitor has fully decided, since it also serves browsing-stage visitors who aren't ready to post yet.
- Testimonials and stats are homeowner-filtered specifically (not the mixed homepage carousel) — proof from people in an identical situation converts better than generic platform-wide proof.
- FAQ is shown as a short accordion preview (4 questions) rather than the full FAQ page — enough to resolve the last-mile objection without making the page feel like a support center.
