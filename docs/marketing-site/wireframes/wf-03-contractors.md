## Hierarchy & conversion notes

- The "Problem With Other Platforms" section is placed second, immediately after the hero — for contractors specifically, naming the shared-industry frustration (paying for low-quality shared leads) before pitching features builds rapport faster than leading with a feature list, since it's the single biggest object contractors bring to any new platform.
- Equal visual weight to the homeowner page's equivalent sections (same card grid, same 3-step stepper pattern) — a contractor should never perceive this page as the "secondary" audience treatment.
- Trust Score gets a dedicated section here (not just a mention) because it's the feature most directly tied to contractor motivation — it's marketed as something that helps good contractors stand out, not a compliance hoop.
