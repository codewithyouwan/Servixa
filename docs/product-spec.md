### **Vision** {#vision .unnumbered}

## **Building the World\'s First AI-First, AI-Native Construction Marketplace** {#building-the-worlds-first-ai-first-ai-native-construction-marketplace .unnumbered}

Our vision is to transform the global construction industry by creating
the world\'s most intelligent, trusted, and connected construction
marketplace, powered by artificial intelligence from the ground up.

We are not simply digitizing construction; we are reimagining how
homeowners, service providers , contractors, suppliers, architects,
designers, and developers discover, plan, execute, and manage
construction projects in an AI-native ecosystem.

### **Phase 1: Revolutionizing Residential Construction in the United States** {#phase-1-revolutionizing-residential-construction-in-the-united-states .unnumbered}

We begin by addressing one of the largest markets in the world, the U.S.
residential service ,construction industry.

Our AI-powered marketplace will seamlessly connect homeowners with
verified service providers and contractors for home services ,
renovations, remodeling, repairs, maintenance, landscaping, roofing,
HVAC, plumbing, electrical work, painting, and new home construction.

Our goal is to become the most trusted platform for residential
construction and services across the United States before expanding into
international markets.

### **Phase 2: Global Expansion** {#phase-2-global-expansion .unnumbered}

Once our residential platform reaches scale in the U.S., we will expand
into major international markets, adapting the platform to local
regulations, contractor ecosystems, languages, currencies, and customer
expectations.

Our long-term ambition is to become the global operating system for
residential construction, enabling millions of homeowners and
construction professionals to collaborate more efficiently through
AI-driven workflows.

### **Phase 3: Commercial Construction Marketplace** {#phase-3-commercial-construction-marketplace .unnumbered}

Building on our residential success, we will launch a second module for
commercial construction to serve businesses, developers, property
owners, general contractors, government agencies, and enterprise
clients.

Purpose-built AI agents will support estimating, procurement, contract
administration, project management, scheduling, quality assurance,
safety monitoring, and operational decision-making, enabling
organizations to deliver projects faster, more efficiently, and with
greater transparency.

### **Our Long-Term Vision** {#our-long-term-vision .unnumbered}

We envision a future where every construction project begins with AI.

Whether renovating a single kitchen, building a custom home, managing a
commercial office development, or delivering a large-scale
infrastructure project, our platform will intelligently connect every
stakeholder, automate routine tasks, provide predictive insights, and
simplify the entire construction journey.

By combining artificial intelligence, trusted professionals, data-driven
decision-making, and a seamless digital experience, we aim to become the
world\'s leading AI-native construction marketplace---setting a new
global standard for how construction projects are planned, managed, and
delivered.

Our mission is simple: **to make construction faster, smarter, more
transparent, and more accessible for everyone, everywhere.**

**Monetization strategy**

The basic membership will be free for both homeowners and contractors to
encourage adoption and grow the platform.

As the platform matures, premium features should transition to a paid
subscription model. We can initially keep these premium features free
for the first few years (e.g., up to three years) to build our user
base, and then gradually reduce the free access period as subscriptions
gain traction.

We should design the platform with this future monetization strategy in
mind, ensuring that premium features, subscription plans, billing, and
user access controls are built into the architecture from the outset.

### **Option for Manual Entry by Founders and administrators** {#option-for-manual-entry-by-founders-and-administrators .unnumbered}

The platform should include an option that allows founders or
administrators to manually create and manage contractor profiles.

During the initial launch, when contractor subscriptions and
user-generated listings are limited, founders can manually add verified
contractor information for selected cities and categories. This ensures
the marketplace has sufficient listings, improves the user experience,
and helps build trust from day one.

The manual entry feature should allow founders to:

-   Add contractor profiles by city and service category.

-   Edit or update contractor information at any time.

-   Mark profiles as \"Verified\" or \"Platform Added\" (optional).

-   Easily transition manually created profiles to contractor-managed
    accounts once they register on the platform.

This approach helps bootstrap marketplace supply until organic
contractor onboarding reaches scale.
