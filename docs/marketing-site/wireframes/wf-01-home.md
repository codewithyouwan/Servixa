## Hierarchy & conversion notes

- Exactly one heavy-border CTA is visible in any given scroll position — the hero's "Post Your Project Free" is the only heavy element above the fold; every CTA after it until the final band is outline-weight, so the visual language itself tells the visitor which action matters most.
- The trust bar sits directly below the hero, before any feature explanation — belief is established before the pitch, matching the Stripe logo-bar pattern referenced in `marketing-03-homepage.md`.
- Homeowner and contractor content alternate (Problem/Solution is neutral → Homeowner steps → AI spotlight is neutral → Contractor steps) rather than blocking all-homeowner content together — neither audience scrolls past a wall of "not for me" sections to reach their own.
- The final CTA band uses a full-width, high-contrast treatment (the Linear/Vercel convention) specifically so it reads as the page's closing statement rather than another content section — L-spacing above it reinforces that this is a deliberate stop, not a continuation.
