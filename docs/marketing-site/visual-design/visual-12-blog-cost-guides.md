# Visual Screen Design — Blog / Cost Guide Hub

Follows `visual-00-language.md`. Header and footer identical to `visual-01-home.md`. Covers the hub/index page only.

**Hero + Search.** A 40px headline, "Plan Smarter With Our Project Guides," an 18px subtext, and the same search input construction as the FAQ page's.

**Category filter bar.** A single horizontal row of pill buttons — All, Kitchen, Bathroom, Roofing, HVAC, and so on — each a small rounded pill, inactive ones white with a 1px `neutral-300` border and `ink-900` text, the active one filled solid `ink-900` with white text (this is the one interactive control on the site where the "active" state is marked with ink rather than the accent color, since these are filter toggles rather than the page's primary action — reserving `accent-600` exclusively for genuine calls to action). The row scrolls horizontally on narrower viewports with a soft fade at the right edge signaling more categories exist.

**Featured guide.** One large card spanning nearly the full 1200px width, image on the left in a 20px radius frame, text on the right: a 32px semibold title, "2026 Kitchen Remodel Cost Guide," a short description, and an outline "Read Guide" button — visibly larger and more prominent than the grid cards beneath it, establishing an editorial hierarchy.

**Guide/article grid.** Three columns, 48px gaps, each card a 16:9 photo at the top (12px radius, clipped), a small `#F9FAFB` category pill sitting just above a 20px semibold title beneath the image. A centered "Load More" outline button sits 64px below the grid.

**Browse by Project Type.** Identical construction to the Homeowners page's ten-item icon grid — reused, not redesigned.

**Testimonials.** Two cards, "the guide helped me" framing.

**Statistics.** Quiet number band, three figures (Guides Published, Monthly Readers, Categories Covered).

**Final CTA band.** Standard `#0A0A0A` full-bleed, dual-button treatment, headline "Ready to Get Quotes for Your Project?"
