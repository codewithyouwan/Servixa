# Visual Screen Design — Pricing

Follows `visual-00-language.md`. Header and footer identical to `visual-01-home.md`.

**Hero.** Deliberately small — a 40px (not 56px) headline, "Simple, Transparent Pricing," an 18px subtext beneath, roughly 320px of total height. No image, no buttons: this page's job is a fast, calm answer, and the hero should get out of the way quickly rather than perform.

**Homeowner pricing block.** A single card, centered, capped at about 420px wide rather than stretching to the container — this is the page's visual focal point, and holding it narrow keeps it reading as one self-contained answer rather than a grid item. Its border is a full 1.5px `ink-900` line (heavier than the standard 1px `neutral-300` hairline used everywhere else), 12px radius, generous 32px internal padding. Inside: "For Homeowners" in 14px semibold `neutral-700`, then, dominating the card, "$0" set at the full 56px display size in `ink-900` — the single largest piece of type on the page — followed by a short line of 14px `neutral-700` copy and a 48px `accent-600` button, full-width within the card.

**Contractor pricing block.** Three cards in a row, equal width, 48px apart. Two are plain — 1px `neutral-300` border, white fill. The middle "Pro" card is visually promoted: a 2px `accent-600` border instead of 1px gray, and a small pill sitting astride its top edge, centered, reading "Recommended" in white text on an `accent-600` fill. Each card holds a 20px semibold tier name, a large price (40px, one step below the homeowner card's 56px, since $0 remains the page's most important number), a short list of features each preceded by a small `green-600` check, and a button at the bottom — `accent-600` filled on the Pro card, outline on the other two, so only one heavy-weight button is visible across all three cards at once.

**What's Included.** A comparison table, same bordered/12px-radius construction as the AI Features page's table, with the "Pro" column carrying the same 3px `accent-600` top-border treatment as its card above it, so the table and the cards visually agree with each other about which tier is recommended.

**Testimonials.** Two cards, value/ROI-framed quotes.

**Statistics.** Quiet number band, three figures.

**FAQ.** Full accordion list shown directly on this page (not a preview), same construction as elsewhere — continuous rows, 1px dividers, no card wrapper.

**Final CTA band.** Same `#0A0A0A` full-bleed, dual-button treatment as Home's.
