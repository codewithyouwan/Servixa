# Visual Screen Design — Home

Follows `visual-00-language.md`. Structural basis: `wf-01-home.md` / `ui-spec/ui-03-home.md`.

**Header.** A 72px bar, pure white, sitting flush against the hero below it with no shadow or border until the visitor scrolls — at rest it looks like part of the page, not a bar floating above it. The logo sits 120px from the left edge (matching the page's outer margin at this width), a small dark wordmark in `ink-900`. Six nav items follow with generous 32px gaps between them, set in 16px medium-weight type, `ink-900` — quiet, not bold, no underlines except on the current page, where a 2px `accent-600` line sits 8px below the label. "Log In" trails in `neutral-700`, deliberately lighter than its neighbors. The button on the far right is a 48px-tall, `accent-600`-filled pill with 12px corner rounding, white 15px medium-weight label "Post a Project," and nothing else competing with it in this row — it is the only saturated color visible in the entire header.

**Hero.** A tall, airy band — roughly 640px — with 128px of clear white space above the header and below the section that follows. On the left, occupying about 45% of the 1200px content width: a small `accent-600` eyebrow, "AI-POWERED CONSTRUCTION MARKETPLACE," set in 13px uppercase medium type with light letter-spacing, sitting 8px above the headline. Below it, in 56px Inter semibold with tight -0.02em tracking, `ink-900`: "Get Your Home Project Done Right — Without the Guesswork," broken across two lines, generous 64px line-height so the two lines don't feel cramped. Twenty-four pixels below that, an 18px paragraph in `neutral-700`: "Describe your project, let AI build the scope and budget, and get matched with licensed, insured contractors near you." Thirty-two pixels below the paragraph, two buttons side by side with 16px between them: a 56px-tall `accent-600` pill, white text, "Post Your Project Free," and beside it a 56px-tall outline pill — 1.5px `ink-900` border, transparent fill, `ink-900` text — reading "I'm a Contractor." On the right ~55%: a single, naturally-lit, slightly desaturated photograph of a kitchen mid-renovation, filling a 20px-radius frame that runs almost to the container's right edge. Layered over its bottom-left corner, overlapping by about half its own height, sits a small white card — 20px radius, the softest shadow token in the system (barely visible, just enough to read as "above" the photo) — showing a miniature chat-style exchange: a gray message bubble reading "What's your project?", a dark reply bubble "Kitchen remodel," and beneath both, a compact result card with a budget range and a category tag in `accent-600`-on-white pill styling. This card is the one moment in the hero where the product itself is shown working, not just described.

**Trust bar.** A slim band directly beneath, background shifted to the barely-there `#F9FAFB` tint — the only visual signal that a new section has started is this near-imperceptible warmth change, no rule line. Four items sit in a single centered row, each a 24px `neutral-500` outline icon (shield, star, checkmark, lock) followed by 8px of space and a 14px semibold `neutral-700` label: "Licensed & Insured Verification," "4.8/5 Average Rating," "X,000+ Projects Completed," "Secure, No-Spam Posting." Nothing here is a button or a link — it reads, correctly, as a quiet strip of proof to be absorbed in passing, not interacted with.

**Problem → Solution.** Back on white, 64px above and below. A centered 32px semibold headline, "Finding the Right Contractor Shouldn't Feel Like a Gamble," followed 32px later by three cards in a row, 48px apart. Each card: white fill, a 1px `neutral-300` hairline border, 12px radius, 24px padding on all sides, no shadow at rest. Inside: a 28px `neutral-500` icon at the top, 16px below it a 20px semibold `ink-900` line naming the problem ("Unclear scope"), and beneath that a 14px `neutral-700` line completing the thought with a small `green-600` checkmark leading it: "AI builds a structured scope of work." On hover, the card's border darkens to `ink-900` and the soft shadow token appears beneath it — the only motion is a 200ms ease border/shadow crossfade, no scale or bounce.

**How It Works — Homeowners.** A three-step horizontal sequence, each step marked by a 32px dark circle with a white numeral, a 36px outline icon beside it, and a 20px semibold label below ("Describe Your Project," "Get Matched," "Compare & Choose"). A thin 1px line connects the three circles left to right at their vertical center, giving the sequence a visible spine. Centered beneath the steps, a single 48px `accent-600` button: "Post Your Project Free."

**AI Features Spotlight.** Four cards in a row (this section alone breaks the usual three-up rhythm, since there are four distinct capabilities to show), each identical in construction to the Problem→Solution cards but slightly narrower. Below the grid, a wide image split into two halves: the left half sits on the `#F9FAFB` tint and shows a loose block of typed text inside a plain rectangle labeled "Before"; the right half is a crisp white card with the soft shadow token, showing a labeled, structured summary — category, budget range, timeline — clearly the "after." An arrow glyph in `neutral-500` sits between the two halves. Centered below, an outline-style button: "See AI Features."

**How It Works — Contractors.** Visually identical construction to the homeowner stepper above, positioned directly after it, so the two sequences read as intentional mirrors rather than as one primary and one secondary treatment. Its centered button, however, is outline-style ("Join as a Contractor"), keeping the page's single dominant CTA reserved for the homeowner action.

**Testimonials.** Three cards in a row, each: a 40px circular photo, name and city in 14px semibold beside it, a row of five small star glyphs beneath (filled ones in `accent-600` — the one place outside buttons/focus/tabs the accent appears, since a star rating is itself a kind of active-state indicator), then a 16px quote in `neutral-700`, and at the bottom a small `green-600` "Verified Project" tag with a tiny check icon.

**Statistics.** A quiet band on the `#F9FAFB` tint, four numbers in a centered row, each rendered large (40px semibold `ink-900`) with a 14px `neutral-700` label beneath. *(Ships only once real figures exist — see design note in `marketing-03-homepage.md`.)*

**Trust & Verification.** Same card construction as Problem→Solution, three cards, icons in `green-600` instead of the default `neutral-500` — the one section-wide exception that visually clusters "these three things are all part of the trust story."

**Pricing teaser.** A narrow, low-key block — no card, no border — just a centered headline and one line of supporting text, tighter spacing (32px, not 64px) above and below, deliberately reading as a lighter pause in the page rather than a full section.

**Blog/Resources teaser.** Three content cards, each a 16:9 photo at the top (12px radius, clipped to the card), a small pill-shaped category tag in `#F9FAFB` background with `ink-900` text sitting just above the card's 20px semibold title.

**Final CTA band.** Full-bleed, edge to edge, background `#0A0A0A` — the single darkest surface on the page — with 128px of padding above and below its centered content. A 32px semibold white headline, "Ready to Get Started?", a 18px `neutral-300` line beneath it, and two 56px buttons: `accent-600` filled with white text, and a white-outline/white-text ghost button beside it. This band is the visual bookend to the hero — the only other place on the page using large type and paired buttons at full weight.

**Footer.** Background `#0A0A0A`, five columns of 14px links in `neutral-300` under 14px semibold white column headers, a hairline `ink-800` rule above the bottom bar, which holds copyright text, three small social glyphs, and a short trust strip (shield + lock icons with one-line labels) — the very last thing a visitor sees on the page is one more quiet restatement of "this is verified and secure."
