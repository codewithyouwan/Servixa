# Visual Screen Design — For Contractors

Follows `visual-00-language.md`. Footer identical to `visual-01-home.md`.

**Header.** Identical to Home's header in every respect except the right-hand button, which swaps to "Join as a Contractor" — same 48px `accent-600` pill, same position.

**Hero.** Same left-text/right-image construction as the Homeowners hero, mirrored for this audience: single 56px `accent-600` button, "Join as a Contractor," no second button. The photograph shows a contractor genuinely mid-task — hands on a tool, real work rather than a posed handshake — and this page's floating card (unlike Homeowners') sits over its bottom-left corner: a compact white notification card, 20px radius, soft shadow, showing a small `accent-600`-dot "New Lead" indicator beside a one-line project summary — the concrete proof-of-product moment for this audience.

**The Problem With Other Platforms.** A single centered block, no cards — a 32px semibold headline, "Tired of Paying for Leads Everyone Else Already Has?", followed by one paragraph of 18px `neutral-700` copy, its line length capped at roughly 720px so it never stretches uncomfortably wide even at full container width.

**What You Get.** Four cards, identical construction to the feature cards used throughout — Matched Leads, Mini CRM, Portfolio Page, AI Proposal Drafting.

**How It Works (expanded).** Same three-circle stepper as the Homeowners page's expanded version, contractor copy (Get Verified, Receive Matched Leads, Win More Work), each step carrying its own paragraph. Centered CTA: "Get Verified & Start Getting Leads."

**Trust Score Explained.** A horizontal segmented bar, roughly 480px wide and 12px tall, fully rounded ends — filled left-to-right in a gradient of `green-600` easing toward `neutral-300` at the unfilled end, no numbers overlaid on the bar itself. Beneath it, four small labeled ticks in 14px `neutral-700`: Completion Rate, Response Speed, Customer Retention, Dispute History. A 32px semibold headline sits above the whole thing: "Your Reputation, Backed by Real Data."

**Pricing preview.** Same low-key, cardless, tightly-spaced treatment as Home's pricing teaser, ending in an outline link: "See Contractor Pricing."

**Testimonials — contractor-filtered.** Two cards, same construction, attribution lines carrying trade + city ("Mike R., Electrician — Austin, TX").

**Statistics.** Same quiet number band, contractor-relevant figures (Verified Contractors, Lead Acceptance Rate, Avg. Response Time).

**FAQ preview.** Identical accordion construction to the Homeowners page, contractor question set.

**Final CTA band.** Same `#0A0A0A` full-bleed treatment, single button: "Join as a Contractor."
