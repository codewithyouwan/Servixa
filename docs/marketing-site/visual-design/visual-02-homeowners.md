# Visual Screen Design — For Homeowners

Follows `visual-00-language.md`. Header and footer are pixel-identical to `visual-01-home.md` — not re-described here.

**Hero.** Same construction as Home's hero — eyebrow, 56px headline, 18px subtext, photo frame on the right — but the button row holds a single 56px `accent-600` pill ("Post Your Project Free") with no outline button beside it; on this audience-locked page, a second, contractor-facing button would visually compete with a page that has exactly one job. The photo itself is a tighter, warmer crop — a kitchen or bathroom mid-renovation, chosen for specificity over the more general "contractor at work" framing used elsewhere — and carries no floating UI card, since that proof point belongs to Home and to the AI Features page.

**Why Homeowners Choose Us.** Four cards in a row, identical construction to Home's feature cards (white fill, hairline border, 12px radius, 24px padding, 28px `neutral-500` icon, 20px semibold title, 14px `neutral-700` description) — titled AI Project Assistant, Verified Contractors, Compare Quotes Side-by-Side, In-App Messaging & Tracking.

**How It Works (expanded).** The same three-circle, connected-line stepper as Home, but each step now carries an additional 16px `neutral-700` paragraph beneath its title — three or four lines explaining the step in real detail, since a visitor on this page is further into consideration than a Home visitor skimming past. Centered beneath, one 48px `accent-600` button: "Get Matched With Contractors."

**What You Can Build/Fix.** Ten small items in two rows of five, each a 28px `neutral-500` icon centered above a 14px `neutral-700` label — Kitchen, Bathroom, Roofing, HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing, Flooring, Painting, Landscaping, New Construction. No card, no border around each — just icon-over-label pairs with generous 48px gaps, reading as a clean navigational grid rather than another row of cards. On hover, each label's color deepens to `ink-900` and a very faint `#F9FAFB` rounded-rectangle highlight appears behind the pair.

**Trust section (condensed).** A single quiet row on the `#F9FAFB` tint — three icon-and-label pairs (License Verified, Insurance Confirmed, Trust Score Tracked), then a centered outline-style link, "Learn About Trust & Safety."

**Testimonials — homeowner-filtered.** Two cards, same construction as Home's testimonial card, quotes and attributions drawn only from homeowners.

**Statistics.** Same quiet number band as Home, three figures instead of four, same treatment.

**FAQ preview.** Four accordion rows, each a 56px-tall trigger — 18px semibold `ink-900` question text on the left, a 24px `neutral-500` chevron on the right that rotates 180° when a row opens. Rows sit directly against each other with a 1px `neutral-300` line between them, no gap, no card wrapper — a clean, continuous list. Beneath the fourth row, a centered "See All FAQs" link.

**Final CTA band.** Same `#0A0A0A` full-bleed treatment as Home, single `accent-600` button only: "Post Your Project — It's Free."
