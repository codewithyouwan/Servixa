# Visual Screen Design — Contact

Follows `visual-00-language.md`. Header and footer identical to `visual-01-home.md`. Every section on this page is intentionally quieter and more compressed than elsewhere — speed, not persuasion, is the job.

**Hero.** Minimal: a 40px headline, "We'd Love to Hear From You," nothing else, roughly 240px tall.

**Contact form + Direct info.** A two-column layout, form on the left at roughly 60% width, direct info on the right at 40%. Form fields are plain white boxes with a 1px `neutral-300` border, 8px radius, 16px internal padding, a 14px semibold `ink-900` label sitting above each field (never inside it as a placeholder-only label). The "I am a..." field is a select styled identically to the text inputs, with a small chevron on the right. The submit button spans the full width of the form column, 48px tall, `accent-600` fill. On the right, plain text: support and partnership email addresses in `accent-600` (the only place besides buttons/focus/tabs the accent marks a real link), and a one-line pointer to the FAQ page beneath.

**Trust reinforcement.** A single centered line — one 24px icon, one 14px sentence — no card, no border, the lightest-weight "section" on the entire site.

**Testimonial.** One compressed card: photo, quote, name — no star row, no verified-project tag (this is a support-responsiveness quote, not a project review, so that specific tag is correctly withheld here).

**Statistics.** Two figures shown inline in a single row at a smaller scale (20px, not 40px) than the standard stat band elsewhere.

**CTA band.** The one page on the site whose closing band stays on white rather than switching to `#0A0A0A` — two outline buttons only, "See How It Works" and "Browse FAQs," no filled `accent-600` button at all, since a hard conversion push is the wrong note to end a support-oriented page on.
