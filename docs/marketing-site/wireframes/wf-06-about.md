## Hierarchy & conversion notes

- Unlike every other page, the final CTA band here carries four buttons, not two — About page traffic includes investors, press, and future partners named explicitly in the brief, who aren't ready to post a project or join as a contractor; "Get in Touch" and "See Open Roles" give them a relevant next step instead of forcing them toward an irrelevant CTA.
- The roadmap section is intentionally light-touch (a simple 5-stage marker, not a detailed timeline) — its audience is the same non-homeowner/non-contractor group, and overcommitting to specific dates on a public page creates a liability if the roadmap shifts.
- Team section is flagged as optional and should be omitted entirely rather than shown half-populated — a sparse 2-person grid reads worse than no team section.
