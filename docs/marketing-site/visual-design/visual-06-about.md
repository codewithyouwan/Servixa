# Visual Screen Design — About

Follows `visual-00-language.md`. Header (default dual-CTA) identical to `visual-01-home.md`. Footer identical, except this page's final band is described separately below since it's structurally unique.

**Hero.** No image, no buttons — a 56px headline, "Building the World's First AI-Native Construction Marketplace," a condensed vision statement beneath at 18px, `neutral-700`, roughly 480px of total height. Eyebrow above the headline reads "ABOUT US" in `accent-600`.

**The Problem We're Solving.** A two-column layout, text on the left capped at ~640px for comfortable reading, a supporting photo or illustration on the right in a 20px-radius frame. The text itself is the longest continuous prose passage on the site — a 32px headline followed by two or three paragraphs at 16px, `neutral-700`.

**Our Approach.** Four cards, tighter and simpler than the standard feature card — 16px padding rather than 24px, a 28px `neutral-500` icon, and just a 20px semibold statement beneath it, no body paragraph, since these are meant to be read quickly as a set of principles rather than explained individually.

**Roadmap/Vision teaser.** A quiet horizontal sequence of five small dots (8px circles, `neutral-300`) connected by a thin line, each with a 14px `neutral-700` label beneath — Residential (US), Global Expansion, Commercial Construction, Ecosystem Expansion, Platform Dominance. Deliberately un-bold: no numbered badges, no icons, nothing that implies this is a clickable task list — it's a company trajectory, rendered quietly.

**Team (if present).** Small circular photos (80px), name in 16px semibold, title in 14px `neutral-500` beneath. Per the wireframe's own guidance, this section is omitted entirely rather than shown half-populated.

**Press mentions / testimonials.** A row of grayscale logo marks, each capped at 32px tall, turning full-color on hover — the "as seen in" convention, understated.

**Statistics.** Quiet number band, company-scale figures (year founded, states served) rather than volume metrics.

**Final CTA band.** The one structurally unique band on the site: still the `#0A0A0A` full-bleed treatment, but holding four buttons in a single row rather than two — two `accent-600`-filled ("Post Your Project Free," "Join as a Contractor") and two white-outline ghost buttons ("Get in Touch," "See Open Roles"), 16px apart, wrapping to a 2×2 grid at narrower widths.
