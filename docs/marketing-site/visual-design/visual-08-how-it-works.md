# Visual Screen Design — How It Works

Follows `visual-00-language.md`. Header and footer identical to `visual-01-home.md`.

**Hero + Tabs.** A 40px headline, "How It Works," an 18px subtext, and directly beneath, a two-item tab control: "For Homeowners" and "For Contractors" set in 16px semibold, the inactive tab in `neutral-500`, the active one in `ink-900` with a 2px `accent-600` underline. The whole tab row sits on a 1px `neutral-300` bottom border spanning the container width.

**Flow panel (active tab).** A four-step sequence — one step more than the three-step versions used elsewhere, since this page's flow makes the AI-scope-building step explicit — each step a 32px numbered dark circle, a 36px icon, a 20px semibold label, and now a full paragraph of 16px `neutral-700` copy beneath (this is the most detailed stepper treatment on the site). At desktop widths the four steps sit in a single connected row; the connecting line runs continuously beneath all four circles.

**Behind the Scenes.** Two cards — AI Matching Engine, Verification — identical construction to the standard feature card, positioned directly beneath the flow panel as a distinct, slightly more technical aside.

**Testimonials.** Two cards, process-focused quotes ("From posting to first quote took less than a day").

**Statistics.** Quiet number band, three figures (Avg. Time to First Match, Contractors per Match, Homeowners Who Hire Within a Match).

**Final CTA band.** Standard `#0A0A0A` full-bleed, dual-button treatment.
